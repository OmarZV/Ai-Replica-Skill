
# Product Vision

## Vision

AI Replica Skill transforms a simple prompt into an opportunity to experience how another mind might think, reason, and respond.

Users can choose a real, historical, or fictional character and ask anything—from everyday questions to complex decisions. The AI generates a response modeled around that character's known personality, principles, communication style, experiences, and worldview.

The goal is not simply to imitate how someone **sounds**.

It is to simulate how they might **think**.

---

## The Problem

General-purpose AI assistants are incredibly capable, but their answers often come from a single, generalized assistant perspective.

Users who want a different perspective must manually construct prompts such as:

> "Pretend you are Steve Jobs. Based on his speeches, interviews, leadership philosophy, and approach to product development, tell me how he would..."

This requires users to know how to prompt effectively and still produces inconsistent results.

AI Replica Skill turns that complex prompting process into a product experience.

---

## The Solution

Users provide two simple inputs:

**1. Who do you want to ask?**

Choose a person or character from reality or imagination.

Examples:
- Steve Jobs
- Warren Buffett
- Marcus Aurelius
- Cleopatra
- Sherlock Holmes
- Batman

**2. What do you want to ask them?**

Enter any prompt.

AI Replica Skill builds the context necessary to generate a response reflecting the selected character's perspective.

---

## Core Product Experience

### 1. Choose a Replica

Search or browse a library of real, historical, and fictional characters.

### 2. Ask Anything

Enter a question, situation, idea, or decision.

> "I'm launching a product but customers don't understand why they need it. What would you do?"

### 3. Get the Replica's Response

Receive an answer modeled around the character's:

- Personality
- Communication style
- Known beliefs
- Decision-making principles
- Experiences
- Historical or fictional context

### 4. Understand Their Thinking

Go beyond the answer by surfacing the principles or reasoning that influenced the response.

### 5. Continue the Conversation

Ask follow-up questions while maintaining the same character perspective and context.

---

## Product Principles

### Perspective Over Imitation

The product should capture how someone approaches problems—not merely reproduce recognizable phrases or mannerisms.

### One Prompt Away

Users should never need to understand prompt engineering to create a high-quality character simulation.

### Grounded When Possible

Real-person replicas should draw from reliable source material such as books, interviews, speeches, letters, biographies, and documented viewpoints.

### Transparency Over Deception

AI-generated replicas should always be clearly identified as simulations rather than authentic statements from the person being represented.

### Entertainment Meets Utility

A conversation with Batman can be entertaining. A conversation with Marcus Aurelius can help someone rethink a difficult situation. The platform should support both.

---

## North Star

**Make the world's perspectives accessible through a single prompt.**

AI Replica Skill ultimately becomes a library of minds that users can explore whenever they want a different way of looking at a problem.

Instead of asking:

> "What does AI think?"

Users can ask:

> "How would different people think about this?"

---

## Long-Term Vision

Over time, AI Replica Skill evolves from individual character conversations into a **perspective engine**.

Users could:

- Compare multiple replicas side-by-side
- Ask several characters the same question
- Create debates between opposing perspectives
- Build personalized advisory boards
- Explore historical events through historical figures
- Learn frameworks from experts and thinkers
- Create custom replicas from approved source material
- Speak with replicas through voice
- Combine fictional and real-world perspectives

A founder could eventually ask:

> "Have Steve Jobs, Warren Buffett, and Marcus Aurelius debate whether I should sell my company."

Rather than providing one AI-generated answer, AI Replica Skill would surface **three fundamentally different ways of thinking about the same decision.**

---

## What Success Looks Like

AI Replica Skill becomes the place users go when they don't just want an answer.

**They want a perspective.**
